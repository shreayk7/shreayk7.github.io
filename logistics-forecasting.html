Forecasting route-level uncertainty for an AI logistics simulation

I’m a researcher on an eight-member team at Purdue’s Krenicki Center for Business Analytics and Machine Learning, where I develop coherent probabilistic forecasts for an AI-driven logistics simulation.

The problem

A probabilistic forecast gives a range of likely outcomes instead of a single number, so it shows how uncertain each prediction is. In this project, the forecasts quantify that uncertainty route by route.

What I’m doing

I use temporal convolutions that preserve strict temporal ordering: each forecast only uses information from earlier in time, never later.
