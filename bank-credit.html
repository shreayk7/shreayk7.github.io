Reading twelve years of bank credit across four sectors, for a report on lending health

As a research intern at India’s Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), I analyzed twelve years of banking credit data for the State Level Bankers’ Committee and wrote a report on the lending sector’s financial health.

12

years of banking credit data

4

sectors: agriculture, housing, education and MSME

My role
Research intern
Where
Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Mumbai
When
December 2025 to February 2026
For
State Level Bankers’ Committee

The problem

The analysis was for the State Level Bankers’ Committee and covered four kinds of lending: agriculture, housing, education and MSME (small business). My report looked at how financially healthy the lending sector is across all four.

The data

Twelve years of banking credit data across the four sectors.

What I did

I analyzed the data and wrote a report on the lending sector’s financial health across all four sectors.

During the same internship, I also produced analysis and reports for the Sustainable Development Goals, National Income, Annual Survey of Industries and National Sample Survey units of Maharashtra’s Directorate of Economics and Statistics.
