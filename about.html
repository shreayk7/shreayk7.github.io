About

I studied data science and business analytics at HSNC University in Mumbai, where I was Head Convenor of the Research Cell and student lead for the university datathon.

Along the way I interned twice at Piramal Pharma: first building checks that data volumes matched between internal servers and the cloud, then leading the migration of BI applications to Qlik Cloud. I also led the analysis for a peer-reviewed study of AI-curated educational content, placed second in MoSPI’s Data Visualization Hackathon, and spent three months at India’s Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation analyzing twelve years of bank credit data.

Now I’m at Purdue’s Daniels School of Business on a scholarship, studying for an M.S. in Business Analytics and Information Management and doing forecasting research at the Krenicki Center. I’m looking for data analyst roles.
