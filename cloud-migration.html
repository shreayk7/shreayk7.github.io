Moving a pharma company’s BI apps to Qlik Cloud, with 53% fewer apps

Across two internships at Piramal Pharma, I first built a framework to check that data volumes matched between internal servers and the cloud, then led the migration of BI applications to Qlik Cloud.

53%

fewer BI apps

80%

smaller storage footprint

My role
IT intern, twice
Where
Piramal Pharma Ltd., Mumbai
When
May to July 2024; December 2024 to June 2025
Tools
Snowflake, Amazon S3 (AWS), Qlik Cloud, Qlik Cloud Migration Accelerator

The problem

Piramal Pharma was moving data from its internal servers to Snowflake and Amazon S3, and its BI applications to the cloud. I worked on both, one internship each.

Part 1: checking the data, summer 2024

I designed and built a data validation and reconciliation framework. It checked data-volume parity between the internal servers and Snowflake and Amazon S3, safeguarding data integrity during the migration.

Part 2: moving the BI apps, December 2024 to June 2025

I led the migration of BI applications to the cloud using the Qlik Cloud Migration Accelerator. As part of the migration, I restructured the application portfolio, which cut the number of BI apps by 53% and the storage footprint by 80%.
